# Shopping List App — Installation & Update Guide (English)

This is a free private Android preview, distributed as an APK file.
Current APK: Shopping-List-App-1.1.1-preview.apk.

## First installation

1. Download or transfer the APK to your Android phone and open it.
2. If Android asks, allow installation from the app you are using to open the APK.
3. Tap Install, then open Shopping List App.
4. A fresh installation starts with three empty lists: Home, Weekend House and
   Vacation. Your product catalog is initially empty. Signing into an existing
   account can restore that account's saved lists instead.
5. Tap a list to open it. Press and hold a list to rename or delete it.
6. Tap Add product to search your catalog or create a new product. You can also
   manage products through the upper-left menu (☰) → Product catalog.
7. Choose EN or MK at the bottom of the ☰ menu to change the app language.

## Shared lists

Open ☰ → Account → Continue with Google. For the initial test, give the organizer
only your Google email address so they can check tester access. Never share your
password. Accept list invitations through ☰ → Invitations.
Installing the app does not automatically give you access to someone else's lists.

## Updating the app

The app currently does NOT check for new versions or display an update prompt
when you open it. It does NOT download or install updates automatically.

When the organizer sends you a newer APK:

1. Download the new APK to the same phone.
2. Open that APK file, rather than opening the existing app icon.
3. Android will show its installation screen. Tap Update (the wording may vary by
   phone), and wait for installation to finish.
4. Open Shopping List App again.

Do not uninstall the existing app before updating. A compatible update signed with
the same release key keeps your saved app data. If Android refuses the update,
contact the organizer instead of uninstalling; an older development build may use
a different signing key.

Changes to app features or design require a new APK. Changes to shared-list
products, quantities and purchased status sync over the internet without a new APK.
